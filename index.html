Questions

How long does it take?

Most sites go live in 5 to 7 business days once we have your info. Bigger builds with booking systems take a bit longer, and we'll tell you up front.

Do I own my website?

Yes, completely. The site, the domain, the files. If you ever leave, everything comes with you. No hostage situations.

What if I need changes after it's live?

Care plan members just send a message. Small text and photo changes are usually done the same day. Bigger additions get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Do I have to sign a contract?

Only for the build. Care plans are month-to-month, cancel any time with a week's notice.

I already have a website. Can you fix it?

Usually. Send us the link and what's bugging you, and we'll tell you honestly whether to repair it or replace it.
